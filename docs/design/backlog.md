## The AI's look and name

- Friendly face: crescent Man in the Moon (old, bearded, sleepy profile) from Sarah's sketches.
- Horror face: a deceptive predator, a mantis inside a viper-jaw shell that opens like a flower,
  drawn from lure predators such as the orchid mantis. Idea: the moon is the lure and the predator
  is what's inside.
- Names under discussion: "Man in the Moon" (Story), "LunAtIc" (Horror reveal, with the A and I in
  red and dripping, the rest soft glowing blue). Alternatives: "LunAI", "LullAIby".
- Sarah's motif: a black hole / event horizon (dark pupil with a bright ring, doubling as a camera
  lens, with a point-of-no-return mechanic).
